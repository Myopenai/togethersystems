# Compliance — Policy (EN)

Scope:
- Internal development/release processes, documentation, and artifact generation.

Frameworks (guardrails):
- Privacy: GDPR principles
- Accessibility: WCAG 2.1 AA-oriented
- Security: OWASP ASVS-oriented, signatures/SBOM/attestations

Obligations:
- Evidenced reviews, test logs, build hashes.
- Reproducibility (deterministic pipelines).
- Change management incl. approvals and versioning.
