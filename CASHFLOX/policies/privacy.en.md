# Privacy — Policy (EN)

Purpose:
- Protect personal data in line with GDPR principles (data minimization, purpose limitation, transparency).

Principles:
- Collect only necessary data; define clear purposes and retention periods.
- Data subject rights: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability.
- Security: encrypt data at rest/in transit; least-privilege access.

Procedures:
- Data inventory & records of processing activities.
- Incident handling: reporting paths, timelines, documentation.
- Cross-border transfers: only with adequate safeguards.
